Volunteer Nepali Teacher - Summer 2023

In the summer of 2023, I spent two months volunteering at a school in Pyin Oo Lwin, Myanmar. I taught the Nepali language and literature to about 50 excited students in a summer school program.

We taught the students about Nepali stories, poems, grammar and culture. It was amazing to see how eager the students were to learn and how much they improved in just two months. We also gave them tests to check how well they understood the lessons and to help them do better.

This experience was not just about teaching; it also helped me learn about the local culture and community. The students' hard work and the kindness of the people made this volunteering experience very special and unforgettable.

Manomyr : Depression Detection System

Completed Feb 2026

What is the Depression Detection System?

The Depression Detection System is a multimodal AI framework designed to identify indicators of depression by analyzing text, speech, and facial expressions. It fuses features across modalities and creates a unified prediction pipeline for more accurate mental health assessment.

Core Capabilities

Natural Language Processing for linguistic pattern analysis.
Computer Vision for facial expression feature extraction.
LSTM-based deep learning for temporal and contextual understanding.
Multimodal fusion to deliver robust depression risk indicators.

Tech Stack

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript Backend: FastAPI Database: PostgreSQL AI: BERT, RoBERTa, LSTM, CV, NLP, Polynomial Regression Hosted partly on Hugging Face Spaces
